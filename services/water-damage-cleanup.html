✦ Water Damage Cleanup Guidance Highlights for Buffalo Grove Homeowners

  • Stop the source: the pipe must be repaired or shut off first.
  • Dry fast: mold can begin within 24 to 48 hours on wet materials, according to the EPA.
  • Remove wet items: soaked carpet padding, drywall and insulation often cannot be saved.
  • Use airflow: fans and dehumidifiers help in the days after a leak.
  • Know who does what: a plumber fixes the pipe, a restoration company dries and cleans.

Water damage cleanup after a plumbing leak in Buffalo Grove, IL is just as important as the pipe repair. Water that soaks into drywall, flooring and insulation keeps causing damage until it is dried out.

Step-by-Step Water Damage Cleanup Guidance Process

  1. Stop the waterShut off the supply and make sure the leak is repaired or isolated.
  2. Remove standing waterUse towels, a wet vacuum or pumps if it is safe to do so.
  3. Dry everythingRun fans and dehumidifiers and remove materials that cannot dry.
  4. Check for mold and callLook for musty smells or spots and call a professional if damage is large.

Frequently Asked Questions: Water Damage Cleanup Guidance

How fast can mold grow after a leak?

Mold can begin to grow within 24 to 48 hours on wet materials, according to the EPA.

Can I dry water damage myself?

Small, clean-water leaks can often be dried by homeowners. Large or contaminated water needs a professional.

Who do I call, a plumber or a restoration company?

A plumber fixes the pipe. A restoration company dries and cleans. Ask your provider about both.
