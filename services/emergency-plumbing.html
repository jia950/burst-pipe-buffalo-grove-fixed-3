Frequently Asked Questions: Emergency Plumbing Help

What counts as a plumbing emergency?

Burst pipes, major leaks, sewage backups and any water that is damaging your home right now are common emergencies.

What should I do while waiting for the plumber?

Stop the water if it is safe, keep away from electrical hazards, move belongings and take photos.

How do I choose an emergency plumber?

Ask about credentials and the license number, availability and a written estimate. In Illinois, plumbers are licensed through the Illinois Department of Public Health.
