✦ Main Water Shutoff Help Highlights for Buffalo Grove Homeowners

  • Find it now: locate the valve before an emergency, not during one.
  • Common spots: basements, utility rooms and crawl spaces, often near the front wall or the water meter.
  • Valve types: a lever (ball valve) turns a quarter turn; a round handle (gate valve) needs several turns clockwise.
  • Stuck or leaking: do not force it. A plumber can replace a failing valve.
  • Label it: tag the valve so everyone in the home can find it.

Main water shutoff valve help in Buffalo Grove, IL starts with finding the valve. In many homes the water line enters through the basement wall or floor and the shutoff is close to the meter. In others it is in a utility room or crawl space.

Frequently Asked Questions: Main Water Shutoff Help

Where is my main water shutoff valve?

It is usually in the basement, a utility room or a crawl space, often near the front wall of the house or the water meter.

What if the valve is stuck or leaks?

Do not force it. Call a plumber to repair or replace the valve.

Should I turn the valve once in a while?

Many plumbers suggest exercising the valve now and then so it does not seize, as long as it turns easily.
