✦ Slab Leak Repair Highlights for Buffalo Grove Homeowners

  • Warm spots: a floor that feels warm can point to a hot water line leaking below.
  • Sound of water: running water noises when everything is off are a warning sign.
  • Higher bills: an unexplained rise in water use may mean a hidden leak.
  • Detection first: electronic leak detection can locate the pipe before concrete is opened.
  • Several options: spot repair, rerouting or replacing the line, depending on the case.

Slab leak repair in Buffalo Grove, IL can be hard to spot because the pipe is hidden. Slab foundations are less common than basements in many Illinois neighborhoods, but homes with additions, garage slabs or slab-on-grade areas can still have plumbing in or under concrete.

Why you should not ignore a slab leak

Water under a slab can undermine the soil, damage flooring and feed mold. The longer it runs, the more expensive the repair usually becomes.

Step-by-Step Slab Leak Repair Process

  1. Check your meterWith all water off, a moving meter suggests a leak somewhere in the home.
  2. Look for signsFeel for warm floor spots and look for damp carpet, cracks or pooling water.
  3. Limit useIf you suspect a hot water slab leak, ask the provider whether to shut off the heater supply.
  4. Call for detectionAsk about leak detection, repair options and a written estimate before authorizing any work.

Frequently Asked Questions: Slab Leak Repair

What is a slab leak?

A slab leak is a leak in a water or drain pipe that runs under or inside a concrete floor.

How is a slab leak found?

Plumbers use meter checks, sound and electronic detection to locate the pipe without breaking concrete unnecessarily.

Does a slab leak mean the whole house needs new pipes?

Not always. Depending on the pipe condition, options range from spot repair to rerouting or replacing a section.
